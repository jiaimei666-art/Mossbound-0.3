MOSSBOUND — FRIENDSHIP & MEMORIALS UPDATE

This is the complete game, including all previous weapons, fighting styles,
bosses, biomes, mounts, pets, Admin stories and terrain abilities.

PLAY
Unzip everything into one folder. Open index.html in a modern browser.
On Windows you can double-click START-GAME.bat.
For GitHub Pages, put the five game files together in your published folder.

UPDATE YOUR GITHUB COPY
Replace index.html, game.js, style.css and survivors.js with these versions.
Add the new legacy.js file beside them. Commit all five files together.
Do not delete legacy.js: the new friendship, memorial and save systems use it.

FRIENDSHIP STORIES
Talk daily, give favorite food, and complete each survivor's request to earn
five full hearts. Talk beside them with I and choose Begin personal story.
Your decisions change the supplies, garden or story you share.
Mira: plant a garden; harvest food once each game day.
Rowan: build a real bridge, visit the lookout at x 211, earn traveling gear.
Bram: explore the new mine at x 452, y 58; earn the Moleback mount.
Iona: rescue Elian at x 235; a seventh survivor moves into Buried Lantern.
Suri: fly through three rings in 40 seconds; earn Canopy Wings.
Tavi: share dinner, improve friendships, and unlock daily meals.
Return to your friend after exploration/trial objectives to finish their story.
People shows each story's requirements and directions.

REFUGES
Wren's Perch: Mosswood, rope lift x 161.
Buried Lantern: Tidal Coast, rope lift x 413.
Lastleaf Canopy: Prism Jungle, rope lift x 1858.
Stand near a rope lift and press I to travel. Press I near a person to talk.
A game day lasts 240 seconds of active play. Menus pause time.

NEW COMPANION & GEAR
Moleback: choose it in World after Bram's story; R rides, M cycles mounts.
Q dash digs nearby natural terrain while riding; protected structures stay safe.
Canopy Wings: hold jump while falling for an improved gliding descent.
The wind trial needs airborne movement: bring a flying mount or flight power.

DEATH MONUMENTS
Every death leaves a pixel stone monument at the place you fell, recording
the day and cause. A close friend leaves flowers and a remembrance message.
Press I beside a monument to pay respects: +15 health once per monument.
Memories lists every monument and its coordinates. You keep your equipment.

SAVE YOUR ADVENTURE
Autosave runs about every 20 seconds of safe active play. Save opens manual
save, export, import and previous-backup recovery. Continue appears after reload.
Full saves include terrain, building stations, inventory, companions, boss
progress, friendships, stories, avatar, Admin progress and monuments.
Finish boss encounters, Admin trials, wind trials and active code abilities
before saving. A successful save preserves the preceding save as a backup.
Browser saves belong to that browser/site. Use Export save file, then Import
save file to move progress between devices or from this site to GitHub Pages.
The existing friendship-only browser data is carried into your first adventure.

MAIN CONTROLS
A/D move, Space jump/fly, Shift sprint, Q dash, R mount or style ability,
M cycle mount, F attack, C style/Destroy, R Input, X Overrun with Line of Code,
E craft, G equipment, B world guide, L fighting styles, I interact,
H gather food, T feed mount, V eat, P potion, Escape close/pause.
Codes remain available, including ADMIN33 for the Admin panel.
